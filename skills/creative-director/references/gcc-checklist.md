# GCC execution checklist

Use for any campaign running in the UAE, Saudi Arabia or the wider GCC.
Pick the items that apply and list them as one-line "Local checks" in
the response. These are prompts for verification, not legal advice:
regulations change, so every item ends with "confirm with local counsel
or the regulator". Do not state a requirement as definitive unless it is
quoted from a current official source.

## Language and layout

- Arabic and English written as co-originals by native writers, not
  translated after approval.
- Right-to-left reading changes the layout: eye path, headline position,
  logo placement (bottom right is where an Arabic reader starts), and
  motion direction in film and digital. Design both versions together.
- Choose register per market (Modern Standard Arabic or Gulf dialect)
  and per channel.
- Check any language-prominence rules for advertising in each market.

## Regulators and licensing

- Content standards: UAE Media Council; Saudi General Authority for
  Media Regulation (GMedia, formerly GCAM).
- Paid influencers and creators: UAE requires an advertiser permit from
  the Media Council; Saudi Arabia requires a GMedia Mawthooq licence.
  Brief only licensed creators.
- Promotions, discounts, competitions and prize draws generally need
  permits (for example Saudi Ministry of Commerce; in the UAE, emirate-
  level economic departments such as Dubai DET). Confirm before a price
  or prize appears in creative.
- Claims substantiation: "% off" needs a genuine prior price; superlatives
  and health or efficacy claims need evidence.

## Data and minors

- Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of
  2021 on personal data apply to app sign-ups, CRM and competitions.
  Treat under-18 audiences with extra care: consent, data minimisation,
  no targeting that exploits age.

## Culture and calendar

- Modesty in styling and casting; gender mix and physical contact;
  music use, especially during Ramadan.
- Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha follow the lunar calendar and
  moon sighting. Give dates as approximate and say they need official
  confirmation; never state a single exact date as fact.
- Other anchors: UAE National Day (2 December), Saudi National Day
  (23 September), Saudi Founding Day (22 February), back-to-school
  (check each ministry's published calendar), summer travel season.

## Audience practicalities

- Separate the decider from the payer (teen and parent, employee and
  household) and give each a reason.
- Retail reality: mall approvals for pop-ups and store fronts, evening
  peak footfall in summer, buy-now-pay-later prevalence in e-commerce.
