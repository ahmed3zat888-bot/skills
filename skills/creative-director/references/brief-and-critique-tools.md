# Brief and critique tools

## 1. Creative brief template

Use this exact structure when writing or rebuilding a brief.

```
PROJECT:            [brand, market, campaign name]
BUSINESS PROBLEM:   [one sentence, with a number: e.g. "Grow repeat purchase
                    among first-time buyers from 18% to 25% in 12 months"]
ROLE OF COMMS:      [brand building / activation / both, with intended split
                    and time horizon]
AUDIENCE:           [who, what they do now, what tension stops them doing
                    what we want]
INSIGHT:            [a human truth that creates tension; test: would the
                    audience nod and feel slightly exposed?]
SINGLE-MINDED
PROPOSITION:        [one thing we want them to feel or believe; no "and"]
SUPPORT / RTB:      [facts that make the proposition credible]
BRAND ASSETS:       [distinctive assets that must feature: colour, logo,
                    character, sound, typography, tagline]
TONE:               [3 words max, each with what it is NOT]
CHANNELS:           [lead channel first; where the idea must live]
MANDATORIES:        [legal, regulatory, cultural, brand guidelines, languages]
MEASURES:           [short-term KPIs and long-term brand metrics; how and when
                    they will be measured]
BUDGET / TIMING:    [production and media ranges, key dates]
ASSUMPTIONS:        [list every unverified premise in this brief]
```

Brief quality checks (fail any one and fix before briefing a team):

1. Could a competitor use this brief unchanged? If yes, it is not specific.
2. Is there one proposition, or several hiding in one sentence?
3. Is the insight a truth or merely an observation or a product benefit?
4. Is success measurable, and is the measure appropriate to the role of comms?
5. Can the team read it in two minutes?

## 2. Critique scorecard

Score each criterion 1 (fails) to 5 (excellent). Give a one-line reason.

| # | Criterion | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strategic fit | Does it answer the brief's problem and proposition? |
| 2 | Insight | Is it built on a real human tension, felt by the audience? |
| 3 | Idea | Can it be said in one sentence? Is it surprising yet obvious in hindsight? |
| 4 | Brand linkage | Would the audience know whose ad this is within seconds? Are distinctive assets early and prominent? |
| 5 | Emotional pull | Does it make people feel something, not just know something? |
| 6 | Distinctiveness | Does it break category conventions, or look like everyone else? |
| 7 | Campaignability | Can it run for years and across channels as a platform? |
| 8 | Craft | Writing, art direction, casting, sound, edit: is execution at the level of the idea? |
| 9 | Cultural and legal safety | Any risk with norms, regulators, claims substantiation, or sensitive groups? |
| 10 | Measurability | Is it clear how success will be read? |

Interpretation (practitioner judgement, not a validated instrument):

- Any score of 1 on criteria 1, 4 or 9: **Kill or Rework** regardless of total.
- Total 40 to 50 with no score below 3: **Proceed**.
- Total 28 to 39: **Rework**, name the one big problem.
- Below 28: **Kill**, go back to the brief or insight.

Where budget allows, recommend validating with a pre-test (e.g. System1,
Kantar Link) or an in-market test cell rather than relying on the
scorecard alone.

## 3. Feedback formula for creative teams

1. Objective reminder (one sentence).
2. Verdict (Kill / Rework / Proceed).
3. The one big problem.
4. What to protect.
5. Two or three specific fixes.
6. Deadline for the next round.
