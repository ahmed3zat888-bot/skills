# Debrief rubrics

Scores use a four-point behaviourally anchored scale. Every score
requires evidence: the turn number and a quoted line. No score without
evidence; if the behaviour was not attempted, record "not observed".

| Score | Anchor |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Behaviour absent or counter-productive (for example: discounted in turn 2 when the target was "no discount before exploring needs") |
| 2 | Attempted once, incomplete or late; persona did not move because of it |
| 3 | Performed clearly at least once and contributed to the persona moving |
| 4 | Performed consistently and adapted when the persona escalated |

The anchors are a design judgement intended to reduce scoring
ambiguity; they are not a validated instrument.

## Behaviour banks

Pick two or three per session as target behaviours. Users can add
their own.

### Client conversations

- Opens with a question about the client's purpose before showing
  product.
- Uses known client history without re-asking.
- Asks at least two open questions before proposing.
- Handles a price objection without discounting outside policy.
- Acknowledges a complaint's specific failure before offering a remedy.
- Proposes a concrete next step with a time (hold, call-back, delivery).

### Stakeholder and influence conversations

- States the business problem and its cost in numbers in the first
  two turns.
- Offers options (at least two) instead of a single ask.
- Names the stakeholder's constraint before arguing for the proposal.
- States measurement method and baseline.
- Admits uncertainty explicitly where evidence is missing.
- Closes with an agreed decision, owner and date.

### Coaching conversations

- Contracts on purpose and confidentiality at the start.
- Ratio of questions to statements above 2:1 in the exploration phase.
- Asks open questions (what, how) rather than leading questions.
- Reflects the coachee's words before challenging.
- Lets the coachee generate options before offering any.
- Closes with a specific, owned commitment.

## Debrief template

```
OUTCOME
Persona moved: yes / partly / no
Concession conditions met: [list, met or not]
Hidden interest: [reveal]

SCORES
1. [Target behaviour]: [1-4], turn [n]: "[quote]"
2. [Target behaviour]: [1-4], turn [n]: "[quote]"
3. [Target behaviour]: [1-4], turn [n]: "[quote]"

KEEP: [one specific behaviour]
CHANGE: [one specific behaviour, with what to do instead]

REWRITE (turn [n])
Original: "[quote]"
Stronger: "[rewrite]"

NEXT: rerun at level [x] / move to scenario [y]
```
