# LinkedIn Amplification Network (Ahmed edition)

Employee advocacy programs amplify a company. A personal brand needs the same effect from a different source: other people with credible audiences who choose to share, feature or cite the user's work because it is useful to their audience. This skill designs and runs that network for the user's personal brand only.

## Before you start

1. Load the `ahmed-linkedin-house-rules` skill and apply it. It overrides this file where they conflict.
2. Personal brand only. No employer programs, colleagues as amplifiers, employer channels or employer brand. Collaborators are chosen for the user's own field and audience.
3. No engagement pods, reciprocal-like groups, bought engagement or automation. LinkedIn's Professional Community Policies prohibit inauthentic behaviour, including coordinated activity to inflate engagement. Beyond the policy risk, reach from pods comes from people outside the target audience.
4. No outreach is sent from this skill. The user sends every message.

## When to trigger

"Grow beyond my followers", "who should I collaborate with", "amplification plan", "get featured", "podcast or panel invitations", "advocacy for my personal brand".

## Amplification channels

| Channel | What it is | Effort | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thoughtful comments on target creators | Consistent, specific comments on 5 to 10 creators' posts | Low, ongoing | Visibility to their audience, relationship start |
| Co-authored or collaborative post | Shared post or carousel with a peer, both credited | Medium | Reaching an adjacent audience |
| Guest feature | Writing for another creator's newsletter, or featuring them in the user's | Medium | Depth, credibility |
| Podcast or live appearance | Guest on a show in L&D, leadership, coaching, psychology | Medium to high | Authority, long-form proof |
| Panels and talks | Conference, association, university, community events | High | Authority, offline network |
| Citing and crediting | Referencing peers' work with credit in posts | Low | Reciprocity without asking |
| Communities | Professional associations and practitioner groups the user already belongs to | Low to medium | Trust within a niche |

## Process

1. Goal: ask what the network should do in the next 90 days. Pick one primary outcome: reach a new audience, build authority (talks, podcasts), or find collaborators.
2. Map the bench. Build or reuse the watchlist from `ahmed-linkedin-engagement-watchlist-builder` and `ahmed-linkedin-niche-creator-finder`. Sort people into four rings:
   - Ring 1, peers (similar size, same field): co-creation partners.
   - Ring 2, reach targets (larger audience, adjacent field): guest features, comments.
   - Ring 3, hosts (podcast hosts, newsletter editors, event organizers): appearances.
   - Ring 4, communities: groups and associations.
   Only use people and shows verified from live public posts or pages. Never invent a name, show or event.
3. Pick 8 to 12 targets in total for 90 days. More than that does not fit 2 to 3 hours a week.
4. For each target, define the give first: a specific comment, a credited citation, a useful resource, an introduction. The ask (collaboration, guest spot) comes only after 2 to 3 genuine exchanges.
5. Draft the outreach for the ready targets with `ahmed-linkedin-dm-opener` or `ahmed-linkedin-connection-request-hook`. One personal reason per message. No template blasts.
6. Prepare a one-page speaker and guest sheet: bio (Arabic and English), 3 talk or episode topics from the user's pillars, proof points that are public, a photo note. No employer figures.
7. Set the rhythm inside `ahmed-linkedin-weekly-planner`: 10 minutes for ring 1 and 2 comments on engagement days, one outreach a week, one collaboration a month.
8. Define metrics and review monthly.

## Metrics (from the user's own data only)

- Leading: collaborations agreed, invitations received, replies from targets, new followers in the target audience (from the user's LinkedIn export, where available).
- Lagging: impressions from outside the user's network on collaborative posts, inbound requests (talks, coaching, collaboration).
- Do not set numeric targets from generic benchmarks. Set the baseline in month 1 and compare to it.

## Rules

- Value before the ask. Every target receives at least one useful contribution before any request.
- Credit every collaborator in posts. Never present joint work as solo work.
- Say no to collaborations that conflict with the user's employer obligations or that would read as speaking for an employer.
- Run a pre-mortem before delivering the plan (house rules section 9).
