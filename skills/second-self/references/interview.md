# Profile interview

Run one phase per sitting if the user prefers short sessions. Ask three
to five questions at a time. Record answers in the user's own words and
tag them `[stated]`. Skip questions already answered by confirmed
`[imported]` items.

## Phase 0: Confirm imported items

List every `[imported]` item from existing memory, skills, or
preferences. For each: keep, correct, or delete. Confirmed items become
`[stated]`.

## Phase 1: Role and context

1. Describe your role in two sentences, as you would to a peer outside
   the company.
2. What does a good week at work look like? A bad one?
3. Which three outcomes do you want to be known for in three years?
4. Which parts of your background (pharmacy, HR, L&D, coaching) shape
   how you think today, and how?

## Phase 2: Values and non-negotiables

1. Name three values you act on even when they cost you something.
   Give one example for each.
2. What will you refuse to do at work, whoever asks?
3. Which behaviour in others irritates you fastest? What does that say
   about what you value?
4. When two of your values conflict, which usually wins?

## Phase 3: Decision style

1. Walk through a recent significant decision: what you knew, what you
   assumed, what you chose, what happened.
2. What evidence persuades you? What evidence do you discount?
3. Fast or slow decider? When does that hurt you?
4. Who do you consult before big decisions, by role, and why them?
5. What decision would you make differently today?

## Phase 4: Voice

1. Share five to ten pieces of your own writing: posts, emails, notes,
   in both languages if you write in both.
2. Which words or phrases do you use often? Which do you never use?
3. How do you open and close a message to a senior stakeholder? To
   your team?
4. How does your Arabic voice differ from your English voice?
5. Show one piece of text you wrote that you are proud of, and one
   that does not sound like you.

## Phase 5: Beliefs about the work

1. Three things about learning and development you believe that many
   peers do not.
2. Three common practices in L&D or leadership development you think
   are wrong, and why.
3. What would change your mind on each?
4. What is your view on AI in learning, today?

## Phase 6: Blind spots and pressure

1. What feedback have you received more than once?
2. How do you behave under pressure, as others describe it?
3. What do you postpone? What does postponing protect you from?
4. When are you most likely to be wrong while feeling sure?

## Phase 7: Goals and current questions

1. Goals for the next 12 months, professional and personal, at the
   level you are comfortable recording.
2. The two or three open questions you keep returning to.
3. What should the mirror push you on most?

## After the interview

1. Draft the profile from `profile-template.md`; show it in full.
2. Run calibration: five to ten "would you say this?" statements,
   rated 1 to 5. Revise items rated 3 or below.
3. Record the date, phases completed, and calibration average.
