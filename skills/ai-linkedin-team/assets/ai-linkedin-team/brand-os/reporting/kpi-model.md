## Rules results-roi must follow
- Minimum sample: "Normal" is set only after at least 8 posts. Below 8, report numbers without ranking.
- Above normal: a post counts as above normal when its engagement rate is at least [FILL IN: for example 1.5] times the normal, and it has at least [FILL IN: for example 1,000] impressions.
- Far off target: a monthly KPI below [FILL IN: for example 60] percent of target for 2 months running.
- Freshness: an export older than 14 days is stale and not used.
- Exports: save as brand-os/reporting/linkedin-<what>-YYYY-MM-DD.<ext>, for example linkedin-posts-2026-11-06.xlsx.

## Known gaps
- Reach through shares and screenshots outside LinkedIn.
- Effects that show up later, such as an invitation months after a post.
- Algorithm changes on LinkedIn's side, which shift every number at once.
