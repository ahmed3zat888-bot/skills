# Channel playbook: LinkedIn
> Read by: content-engine, orchestrator.

- Role: build recognition as an evidence-based L&D and leadership voice in Saudi Arabia and the GCC.
- Audience: see voice-guide.md section 2.
- Formats: text-post, carousel-outline (built by the owner in Canva), comment-thread-starter.
- Posts from: personal profile only.
- Good looks like: the main KPI in brand-os/reporting/kpi-model.md.
- Do: one practical method per post, one source, a question that invites practitioners' experience.
- Do not: hashtags, tagging without permission, links in the post body, employer content, engagement bait.
- Comments: answered by the owner personally. Agents never draft replies to named people without a brief.
- Visual identity: Bridge logo (Latin A and Arabic أ with a coral hamza) on carousels and quote cards. Built by the owner.
