# Brief templates
> Read by: content-engine. The orchestrator's brief names one of these formats.
> The content engine only writes formats that have a template here.

## Every brief has
- Reader: from voice-guide.md section 2
- One promise: what the reader gets, in one sentence
- Proof points: IDs from messaging-bank.md (M, R or E)
- Owner material: the owner's own notes from the calendar row, quoted exactly
- Call to action: from messaging-bank.md section 5

## text-post (bilingual)
- Length: Arabic 120 to 180 words, English 100 to 160 words. Total under LinkedIn's 3,000-character post limit.
- Shape: hook line, the problem in one or two lines, the method as 3 to 5 short bullets or short paragraphs,
  the evidence (one source), one closing question or action.
- Order: Arabic block, one blank line, English block.
- Links: none in the post body. [FILL IN: link in first comment, or no links]
- Visual: [FILL IN: none, or your branded quote card with the Bridge logo]

## carousel-outline
- Output: slide-by-slide text only, for the owner to build in Canva with the Bridge visual identity.
- Slides: 6 to 9. Slide 1 hook, slide 2 problem, slides 3 to N-1 one step each, last slide action plus source.
- Words per slide: under 30 per language. Arabic and English on the same slide, Arabic on top.
- Caption: a short bilingual text-post (Arabic 60 to 90 words, English 50 to 80 words).

## comment-thread-starter
- Output: a short bilingual question post designed to start discussion among practitioners.
- Length: Arabic 40 to 70 words, English 35 to 60 words.
- Must include one specific scenario, not a general question.
