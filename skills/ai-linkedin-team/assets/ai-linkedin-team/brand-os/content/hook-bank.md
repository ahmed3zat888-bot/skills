# Hook bank
> Read by: content-engine. Three opening lines per language per post, the strongest pair picked.
> results-roi may add rows to "Hooks that worked" with status `proposed` when kpi-model.md rules are met.
> The owner approves a row by changing `proposed` to `kept`.

## Patterns
| ID | Pattern | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Plain promise | "You get <result> without <pain>." | "You get behaviour change without adding training hours." |
| H2 | Their question | "<The question in their words>?" | "Why does the training work in the room and fail on the floor?" |
| H3 | Common mistake | "Most teams <mistake>. Here is the cost." | "Most teams measure attendance. Here is the cost." |
| H4 | One specific detail | "<A concrete detail> tells you <something useful>." | "The first 30 days after a course decide its value." |
| H5 | Respectful disagreement | "<Common belief> is wrong for <who>." | "More content is the wrong fix for low engagement." |
| H6 | What is inside | "<What they get> in <format>." | "Four questions to test any training request." |
| H7 | Lesson learned | "I <did X> for years. Then <one change>." | Owner experience only, never invented |

## Rules for every hook
- The body delivers what the hook promises.
- Passes tone-rules.md like everything else.
- First line under 110 characters in each language (working assumption, so it shows before "see more" on mobile; adjust after checking your own posts on a phone).
- Never: fake urgency, clickbait numbers, "You won't believe", questions answered with "yes" by everyone.
