## 2. Reader
- Who reads me: HR, L&D, talent and retail leaders in Saudi Arabia and the GCC.
- What they already know: the basics of training and HR practice.
- What they want: practical methods they can apply on Sunday morning, backed by evidence.
- What makes them stop reading: theory with no application, self-promotion, long introductions.

## 3. Arabic version rules
- Written first, natively. Not a translation of the English.
- Modern Standard Arabic, short sentences, executive register.
- Technical terms: Arabic term first, English term in brackets on first use when it helps, for example: نقل أثر التدريب (training transfer).
- No decorative classical phrasing. No religious phrases used as decoration.
