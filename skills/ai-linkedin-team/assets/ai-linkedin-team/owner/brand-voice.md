## Who I sound like
Executive, clear, humble. Simple and human, to the point. Limited emotion that serves the message, never exaggerated.
Respects the reader's intelligence: attention-grabbing but never superficial.

## How I talk to people
First person "I". Address the reader directly as "you". Professional, warm, not casual slang.

## Language and order
Arabic version first, English version immediately after. Each version is written natively, not translated word for word.
Arabic: Modern Standard Arabic, clear and executive, no heavy classical constructions.

## Sentences and format
- Short, direct sentences. Active voice. Focus on action.
- One idea per paragraph. Bullet points allowed for lists inside a post.
- Support claims with data or a named source.
- No hashtags. No semicolons. No em dashes. No markdown, no asterisks.
- No metaphors, clichés, generalizations or setup language ("In a world where...", "Let's talk about...").
- No unnecessary adjectives or adverbs.
- Posts end with one clear question or one clear action for the reader.

can, may, just, that, very, really, literally, actually, certainly, probably, basically, could, maybe,
delve, embark, enlightening, esteemed, shed light, craft, crafting, imagine, realm, game-changer, unlock,
discover, skyrocket, skyrocketing, abyss, not alone, in a world where, revolutionize, disruptive, utilize,

harness, exciting, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, remarkable, it, remains to be seen, glimpse into,

inquiries, ever-evolving, leverage, robust

Note for the voice check: "it", "that", "can" and "may" are banned as filler. If a sentence cannot be
rewritten without them, the draft is held for your decision, not silently rewritten.
