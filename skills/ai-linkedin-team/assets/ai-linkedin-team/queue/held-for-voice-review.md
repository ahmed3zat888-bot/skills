# Held for voice review

Drafts that failed the voice check. Nothing here goes out. Fix the draft yourself and tick
"Fixed: send it on", or tick "Drop it". Then tell Claude: "Process the held items in queue/held-for-voice-review.md."

If the same rule fails often, the rule may be wrong. Fix the rule in tone-rules.md, then run /voice-regression.
